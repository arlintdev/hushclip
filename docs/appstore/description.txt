Hushclip keeps a searchable, encrypted history of everything you copy on your Mac, and brings it back with one shortcut.

Press ⌘⇧V anywhere to open a fast, good-looking overlay. Type to search, use the arrow keys to pick, and press Return to paste straight into the app you were using.

SORTED AUTOMATICALLY
Every clip is labelled as you copy it, using rules that run entirely on your Mac:
• Screenshots, images and files
• Passwords and secrets such as API keys, tokens and private keys
• Card numbers (checked with the Luhn algorithm)
• Links, email addresses, phone numbers and IP addresses
• Colors, shown with a live swatch
• Code, shell commands, JSON, UUIDs, hashes, file paths and numbers
Press Tab to flip through categories, or just start typing.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your history is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it touches the disk.
• The key is created on your Mac and kept in your Keychain. It never leaves this device.
• Passwords, keys and card numbers are masked on screen (hold Option to peek) and deleted automatically after an hour, or a time you choose.
• Items that password managers mark as concealed are labelled as passwords, or skipped entirely if you prefer.
• No network access, no accounts, no analytics. Nothing is ever collected.

FAST AND KEYBOARD-FIRST
• ⌘1–⌘9 pastes one of your nine most recent items
• ⌘P pins an item so it never expires
• ⌘Return copies without pasting
• ⌘Delete removes an item

NATIVE AND LIGHT
Hushclip is written in Swift, lives quietly in your menu bar, and uses almost no resources. It is free and open source.

Optional: allow Accessibility access so Hushclip can paste for you. Without it, Hushclip copies the item and you press ⌘V yourself.
