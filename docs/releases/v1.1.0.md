## Clipster is now Hushclip

Same app and same encrypted history, with a new name, a new icon, and proper signing.

- **New name:** Clipster is now **Hushclip**. Your existing history moves to the new data folder automatically on first launch, and your Keychain key keeps working.
- **New icon:** a stack of clip cards on deep indigo glass.
- **Signed and notarized:** downloads are signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so there's no more "can't be verified" warning or Open Anyway step.
- **Mac App Store:** Hushclip has been submitted to the Mac App Store. That version runs in the App Sandbox.
- **Auto-paste permission:** Hushclip now checks the post-event permission instead of full Accessibility trust. It still appears under Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

**Upgrading from Clipster 1.0:** quit Clipster, delete `Clipster.app`, and install `Hushclip.app`. The first time Hushclip reads your key, macOS may ask to let it use "Clipster vault key". Click **Always Allow**.
