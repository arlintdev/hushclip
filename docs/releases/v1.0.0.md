The first release of **Clipster**, an encrypted clipboard history for macOS.

### Highlights
- **⌘⇧V overlay**: search, filter, preview and paste anything you've copied, from any app, without leaving the keyboard.
- **Automatic categories**: regex rules sort clips into Screenshots, Images, Files, Passwords, Secrets (API keys, tokens, private keys, JWTs), Cards (Luhn-checked), Links, Emails, Phone numbers, IPs, Colors, UUIDs, Hashes, JSON, Code, Commands, Paths, Numbers and Text.
- **Encrypted at rest**: history and images are sealed with AES-256-GCM. The key is random, created on your Mac, and kept in your login Keychain.
- **Sensitive by default**: passwords, secrets and cards are masked (hold ⌥ to reveal) and auto-deleted after an hour (configurable). Items marked concealed or transient by password managers are respected.
- **Native**: Swift, AppKit and SwiftUI. Universal binary (Apple silicon + Intel), macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

### Install
Download `Clipster-1.0.0.dmg`, drag Clipster to Applications, and open it. See the README for first-launch steps if macOS says the app can't be verified.
