## Touch ID lock

Hushclip can now lock your clipboard history and unlock it with Touch ID.

- **Turn it on** in Settings → Lock → **Lock history with Touch ID**. Macs without Touch ID use an Apple Watch or your Mac password.
- **Choose how long it stays unlocked:** until the screen locks or the Mac sleeps, or for 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 6 hours or 12 hours since you last used it. Hushclip always starts locked after a restart.
- **Locked means locked:** Hushclip saves, then clears the decrypted history and its key from memory.
- **Still recording:** clips you copy while locked are sealed with public-key encryption (HPKE). Only the private key inside your encrypted vault can open them, and they appear in your history when you unlock.
- **Lock Now** in the menu bar locks immediately. Turning the lock off requires Touch ID too.
